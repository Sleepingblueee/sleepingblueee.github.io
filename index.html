About me

Hi there! I am a first-year M.A. student in Emerging Media Studies at Boston University. I currently work at the CRSI Lab, directed by Prof. Xiaoya Jiang.

I am interested in using computational methods to understand public perceptions of and attitudes toward the growing use of AI, particularly AI-generated audio and video, and to measure how effective these tools are in real-world use.

Previously, I received my B.S. in Digital Social Science from Hong Kong Baptist University. During my undergraduate studies, I worked for more than three years with Prof. Johnston Hong-Chung Wong on research into the social integration of Hong Kong youth in mainland China. My undergraduate thesis, advised by Dr. Qian Guo (now at Southern University of Science and Technology), used computational methods to analyze recent changes in cultural heritage–related policies on TikTok.

In addition, I worked for more than a year as a product manager at internet platforms such as TikTok during my undergraduate years, where I gained extensive hands-on experience with products from the user’s perspective. If you are interested in research on product tools or on how people perceive AI, I would love the opportunity to collaborate with you!
